Grey Heron

Ardea cinerea

Gray Heron

Order: Pelecaniformes

Family: Ardeidae

A grey heron with a black cap and white streaked belly. In breeding plumage has plumes emerging from the head. Often seen loafing around to rest, or standing on the edge of water bodies to wait for unassuming prey.

location_on UK, HK
Difficulty to photograph
Stays very still, but one cannot get too close.
