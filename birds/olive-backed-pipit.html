Olive-backed Pipit

Anthus hodgsoni

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Motacillidae

An olive green pipit, with a minimally streaked back and a strongly streaked breast. Face is strongly marked with a faint eye ring and eye stripe, and sometimes there will be a pale patch by the ear. Could be found on the ground, but also sometimes in trees unlike other pipits.
