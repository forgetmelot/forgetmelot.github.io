Common Firecrest

Regulus ignicapilla

A tiny, hyperactive bird. White supercilium and black eye stripe, with a bright orange crest.

location_on Switzerland
Difficulty to photograph
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
Tiny, moves constantly, is gone before you blink.
Difficulty to identify
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
Unlikely to be confused with other birds.

Identification tips

The Common Firecrest has a white supercilium and a black eye stripe, while Goldcrest lacks.
