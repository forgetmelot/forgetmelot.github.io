Brown-necked Raven

Corvus ruficollis

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Corvidae

A large corvid with a brown neck (hard to discern from a distance). Prefers desert habitats. Mostly found as singles or pairs, but could gather in large groups around food sources. Unsurprisingly, a very reliable place to see them is *drumroll*... a rubbish dump.
