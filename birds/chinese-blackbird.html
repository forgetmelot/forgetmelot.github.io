Chinese Blackbird

Turdus mandarinus

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Turdidae

A medium-sized dark thrush, essentially the same as Eurasian Blackbird. Male is black; female is brown. Adults have yellow eye ring and bill, juveniles are more streaked with browner bill. This one is likely a juvenile female.
