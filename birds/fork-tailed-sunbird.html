Fork-tailed Sunbird

Aethopyga christinae

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Nectariniidae

A small bird with a long curved bill. Male has an iridescent turquoise crown, black face and red throat. Back is olive green. The "fork" in the tail is the extension of tail feathers.

Female is overall olive green with paler underparts. Found in woodlands, around flowers where it feeds on the nectar.
