Common Reed Warbler

Acrocephalus scirpaceus

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Acrocephalidae

A small brown bird with a white eye-ring. Buffish breast. Toes are long for grabbing reed. Reed warblers in general have longer bills than other warblers, though it is not very apparent in this image.

location_on Europe
Difficulty to photograph
Quite skulky, more often heard than seen.
