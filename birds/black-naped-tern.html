Black-naped Tern

Sterna sumatrana

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Laridae

White tern with black bill and feet. Black crescent on the nape is akin to an older person with hair loss. Breeds in HK waters in summer, can be found in large numbers. Chases boats for stirred up small fish.
