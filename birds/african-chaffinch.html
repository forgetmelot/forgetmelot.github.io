African Chaffinch

Fringilla spodiogenys

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Fringillidae

North African endemic. Similar to Common Chaffinch, but green colour of the rump extends to the back. Male has an entirely grey head and no orange cheek patch.

Female is overall beige.

location_on Morocco
Difficulty to photograph
Perches on tall branches to sing. White tail edges are easy to spot.
