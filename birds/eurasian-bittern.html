Eurasian Bittern

Botaurus stellaris

Order: Pelecaniformes

Family: Ardeidae

A large, cryptically plumaged heron. Found in dense reedbeds where they are very difficult to see. Neck can be extended to give a reed camouflage. Male gives a booming call in breeding season, which researchers have used to estimate population sizes.
