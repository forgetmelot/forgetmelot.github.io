Eurasian Curlew

Numenius arquata

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Scolopacidae

Largest European wader. Body is brown and streaked, with a long downcurved bill. Female has a longer bill than male. Found in wetlands and coastal areas; breeds in the uplands.
