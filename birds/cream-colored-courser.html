Cream-colored Courser

Cursorius cursor

Cream-Coloured Courser

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Glareolidae

A sandy yellow and very smooth-looking wader. Bill is slightly curved downwards. This is a juvenile, which has a less obvious white supercilium and lacks the black stripe extending from the back of the eye.
