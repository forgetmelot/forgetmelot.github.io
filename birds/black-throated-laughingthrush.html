Black-throated Laughingthrush

Pterorhinus chinensis

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Leiothrichidae

A plump, pear-shaped bird with a long tail, dark grey head and brown back. Throat black, cheek patches white (not present in some subspecies). Occurs in social groups. A popular caged bird.
