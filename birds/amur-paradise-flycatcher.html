Amur Paradise Flycatcher

Terpsiphone incei

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Monarchidae

A bulbul-sized flycatcher with a black head and short crest. Rufous morph has a bright reddish body. Makes flycatcher-like looping motions while foraging.

Under sunlight, the crest has iridescent scale-like patterns.
