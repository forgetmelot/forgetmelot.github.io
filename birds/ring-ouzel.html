Ring Ouzel

Turdus torquatus

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Turdidae

A blackbird-sized thrush with a white crescent on the breast. Male is black with a white crescent, and silvery edges on wing feathers. Female has a cream-coloured crescent that is less obvious.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
Hides among blackbirds and is quite shy.

Watching Tips

It would be best to search for them in the uplands during breeding season, but they can be seen anywhere during migration.
