Far Eastern Curlew

Numenius madagascariensis

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Scolopacidae

A massive brown wader with a long curved bill that is longer in the female. Unlike what is suggested by the name, is not actually found in Madagascar.

Has a brown rump and browner underwings. Perhaps due to its large size, its attention is also drawn to a larger area. Hence, one is unable to approach it as say, a plover.
