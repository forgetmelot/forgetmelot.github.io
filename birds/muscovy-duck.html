Muscovy Duck

Cairina moschata

Order: Anseriformes

Family: Anatidae

A duck with red bare skin around the eyes. This one is of domestic variety, so I suppose I cannot say too much about the species. Domestic ducks tend to come in more colours and are generally larger. Wild Muscovies have black plumage.
