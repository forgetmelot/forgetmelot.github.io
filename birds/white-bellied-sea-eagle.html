White-Bellied Sea-Eagle

Icthyophaga leucogaster

Order: Accipitriformes

Family: Accipitridae

An overall white eagle with a dark grey back and wings. Uncommon resident in HK, can be found along the coast. Crowds gather at Sai Kung Public Pier for the chance that the Eagle would come close to fish. I have been there a couple times, and this is by far the best photo I got. The bird was perched on an island for 3 hours... and I could only leave when the light got too low for any decent pictures.
