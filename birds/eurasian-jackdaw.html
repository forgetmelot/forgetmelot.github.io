Eurasian Jackdaw

Coloeus monedula

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Corvidae

A small crow with a grey nape and silver irises.

Like other crows, Jackdaws seem to be opportunistic feeders. Here they are feeding on a rabbit which may have been predated or died naturally.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
Fairly common and not too skittish.

Identification tips

The most distinctive feature is its silver eyes, which are all black in other crows in the area. In flight, I tend to listen for their calls.

Sounds

Almost says its name with Gnak! and Caw~ calls.
