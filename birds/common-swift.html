Common Swift

Apus apus

A dark swift, white throat patch. Scythe-like wings longer than the body, making the bird larger than it actually is. Does most things on the wing, including but not limited to feeding, drinking, mating, and sleeping; lands only to nest. They are such aerial specialists that their feet are relatively weak, and grounded swifts struggle to take off. Call is a high-pitched scream. Nests in buildings and cliffs.

They have such delicate beaks but such a big gape. This mouth is a void. It swallows everything and anything...
