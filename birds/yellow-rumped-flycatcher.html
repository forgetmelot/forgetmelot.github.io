Yellow-rumped Flycatcher

Ficedula zanthopygia

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Muscicapidae

A sexually dimorphic flycatcher. Female and immature are yellowish green with a bright yellow rump and white wing bars. In HK, adult males are rarer as most birds in passage are first-winters.

location_on HK
Difficulty to photograph
Often found in shady forest.
