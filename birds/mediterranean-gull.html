Mediterranean Gull

Ichthyaetus melanocephalus

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Laridae

A smaller gull with a crimson-red beak and white wing tips. In breeding plumage, head is black with white eye crescents.

location_on Spain
Difficulty to photograph
Not hard to photograph, but hard to find one among many Black-Headed Gulls.
