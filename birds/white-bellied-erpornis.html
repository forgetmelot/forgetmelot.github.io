White-bellied Erpornis

Erpornis zantholeuca

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Vireonidae

A white-bellied bird with yellowish green upperparts and a triangular crest. The only member of its genus; not really related to yuhinas despite the similar appearance, but more related to vireos. Forages quietly in the canopy.
