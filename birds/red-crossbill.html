Red Crossbill

Loxia curvirostra

Other names: Common Crossbill

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Fringillidae

A unique big finch with crossed mandibles that help pry open cones to extract seeds. Specific to pine forests. Male is red-orange.

Female is greenish-yellow.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
Lives on tall conifers, often giving distant views. The best time to photograph them should be when they come down to drink.

Watching tips

Listen for their flight calls! The best way to see them is when they land on or leave trees. At other times they would be foraging deep within the conifers, rendering them hard to see. One could also watch ponds for drinking birds.
