Common Chiffchaff

Phylloscopus collybita

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Phylloscopidae

A plain, olive-green leaf warbler with a pale supercilium. Common garden bird. In the UK is a common summer visitor, and increasing number have been overwintering, possible due to a warming climate.
