Brant

Branta bernicla

Other names: Brent Goose

Order: Anseriformes

Family: Anatidae

A black goose with a black head and neck. White patch on the side of the neck, kind of like a loose Rainbow Loom necklace (I suppose this comment has exposed my age lol). Found in coastal wetlands where they forage. This flock is black-bellied. Another pale-bellied type has a cream-white belly.
