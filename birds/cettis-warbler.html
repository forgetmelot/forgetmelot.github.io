Cetti's Warbler

Cettia cetti

Fat, dumpy brown warbler with a white supercilium and eye ring. Tail is often cocked like a wren. Back is a distinctive brown, something like #996720. Belly is a bluish off white, and greyer towards the junction with the brown. Prefers damp bushes and reedbeds.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
A frustrating species! It emits a loud, explosive call whenever one enters its territory, but it is often than not seen. Even when singing, it hops around instead of perching :(

Watching Tips

Trying to look for one singing bird would generally be quite thankless. But, your chances increase in places where they are more abundant and singing to each other. Look for a small round brown object twitching about in the shrubs or reeds. Be patient!
