Eurasian Siskin

Spinus spinus

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Fringillidae

One of my favourite finches. Overall yellow with obvious wing bars. Female is paler and highly streaked.

Male is brighter yellow with a black cap and bib. Has less streaking on the breast and head.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
Small and quick. Feeds in flocks, often large and loud.

Watching Tips

Often found in alder trees, feeding on the cones along European Goldfinch and Redpoll.
