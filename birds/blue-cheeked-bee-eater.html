Blue-cheeked Bee-eater

Merops persicus

Order: Coraciiformes

Family: Meropidae

A colourful bee-eater. Overall lime green, with a blue gradient around the black eye stripe. Throat is rufous.

Underwings are yellowish orange.

location_on Morocco
Difficulty to photograph
Found perching on top of bushes or trees, or on telephone wires. Green colour does give some camouflage.
