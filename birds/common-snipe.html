Common Snipe

Gallinago gallinago

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Scolopacidae

A brown wader with a long straight bill and cryptic plumage. Probes the mud for food.

One might find a snipe circling the sky while making a bleating, almost mechanical call. This is a display flight known as winnowing.

Has a thick white trailing edge and somewhat pale underwings.

Snipes are weird. They have eyes that are set high on the face so they can look out for predators while probing the mud. They can also move the tips of their beaks independently. At times they seem to not mind people at all. At other times one flushes it before seeing the bird, and then it will fly away with an erratic zigzag trajectory.
