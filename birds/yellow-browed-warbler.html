Yellow-browed Warbler

Phylloscopus inornatus

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Phylloscopidae

A tiny lead warbler with a yellow brow (which many leaf warblers do, to be fair), two wing bars and a greyish bill with a yellow base to the lower mandible.
