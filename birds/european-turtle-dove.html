European Turtle-Dove

Streptopelia turtur

Order: Columbiformes

Family: Columbidae

A small, pinkish brown dove. Wing feathers are black with coppery edges. Pinkish bare skin around the eyes, neck patch is black with four oblique white stripes. They are quite a common sight in Morocco, but have grown increasingly rare in Europe, due to poaching and habitat loss.

Juvenile lacks the neck patch.

Sounds

The name turtur describes the purring tur~ tur~ calls they make.
