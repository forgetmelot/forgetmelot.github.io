Desert Lark

Ammomanes deserti

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Alaudidae

A brown lark, in some angles, and perhaps lighting conditions, reddish. Bill is long and yellow.

location_on Morocco
Difficulty to photograph
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
Can be camouflaged, but makes rather obvious movements.

Watching Tips

Found in stony desert. Sometimes gathers around wells, where nomads pull up water for their animals, to drink.
