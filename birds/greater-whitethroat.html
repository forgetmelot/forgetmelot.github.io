Greater Whitethroat

Curruca communis

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Sylviidae

Brownish-grey warbler with a white throat. Whitish eye-ring and rusty edges on wing feathers. Male is more greyish than female, sings a scratchy songwhile perched on a prominent location. Also has display flight where the bird flies up and then drops down.
