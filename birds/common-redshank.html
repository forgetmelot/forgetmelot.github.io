Common Redshank

Tringa totanus

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Scolopacidae

A brown wader with red legs. Bill is straight, medium-length, has a red base and black tip. Somehow for me, Redshank is not always an option that comes to mind when doing wader ID.

location_on Eurasia
Difficulty to photograph
Also known as the "sentinel of the marsh". Gives loud calls when it sees humans, causing all the wildlife to scatter.

Similar species

Spotted Redshank

  • Taller, more elegant shape
  • Longer bill, downcurved at the tip (vs shorter straight bill)
  • Always has dark upper mandible (vs red base of upper mandible)
  • Breeding plumage is easily distinguishable
