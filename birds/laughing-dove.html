Laughing Dove

Spilopelia senegalensis

Order: Columbiformes

Family: Columbidae

A small dove with a unique ruddy pink colour, something like #BF8273. Wings are a slightly greyer colour. Tiny black squares are visible on the throat when calling. Faces competition from the larger and more aggressive Eurasian Collared-Dove.
