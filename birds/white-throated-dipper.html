White-Throated Dipper

Cinclus cinclus

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Cinclidae

A plump, brown, thrush-like bird with a white throat and breast. Only found in the cleanest fastest-flowing rivers and streams. Dippers are the only songbirds to swim underwater. Bobs the entire body, which gives the name "dipper".

Aside from aquatic invertebrates, also eats fish.

Difficulty to photograph
Can be difficult to spot amidst similar-coloured rocks, and flies away with whirring wings. But otherwise appears quite consistently in the same places.
