Great Grey Shrike

Lanius excubitor

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Laniidae

A black-masked shrike with a grey back. This one is a juvenile with a paler mask and bill. The Latin name excubitor describes its habit of perching on high places to scan for predators or prey.
