Common Crane

Grus grus

Order: Gruiformes

Family: Gruidae

A grey crane. The tallest bird in the UK, around the size of a child. Did you know? The red patch on its crown is featherless, and the red colour comes from the blood supply. Some reintroduction projects have been bringing back cranes to breed in the UK.
