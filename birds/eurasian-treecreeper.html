Eurasian Treecreeper

Certhia familiaris

A small mottled brown bird with a long curved bill and white belly. It moves up tree trunks but unlike nuthatches cannot go down head first, and flies to the base of another tree to start again.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
Well camouflaged, high up in trees. Moves in a spiral pattern and disappears to the other side just when your camera gets ready.
Difficulty to identify
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
All treecreepers essentially look the same. In the UK, Eurasian is the only species. In other parts of Europe, Eurasian is best distinguished from the Short-Toed Treecreeper by voice.
