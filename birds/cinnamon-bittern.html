Cinnamon Bittern

Botaurus cinnamomeus

Order: Pelecaniformes

Family: Ardeidae

An elusive, small heron with a cinnamon-coloured body. When in flight is an attractive flash of ochre.

Often hides among reeds and vegetation, extending its neck and pretending to be part of the scene.
