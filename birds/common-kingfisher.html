Common Kingfisher

Alcedo atthis

Order: Coraciiformes

Family: Alcedinidae

A small blue and orange bird commonly found near rivers and lakes. Female has an orange lower mandible. Dives to fish, usually seen as a flash of turquoise zooming past the water surface. Call is high pitched and easily missed unless one is tuned in to it.

Male has an all-black bill. Like other members in the order Coraciiformes, Kingfishers have syndactyly, in which the 3rd and 4th toes (middle and outer front toes) are fused together.

Juvenile is duller and has a brownish breast.
