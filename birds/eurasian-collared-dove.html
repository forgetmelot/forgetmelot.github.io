Eurasian Collared Dove

Streptopelia decaocto

Order: Columbiformes

Family: Columbidae

A grey-brown dove with red eyes. A black collar with white edges sits on the back on the neck. Found in open country, but also in urban areas in some European cities.

location_on Eurasia
Difficulty to photograph
Sits still for a photo.

Sounds

A three-note call. Guu-guu, gu! Guu-guu, gu!... As if repeating "collared dove".
