Plaintive Cuckoo

Cacomantis merulinus

Order: Cuculiformes

Family: Cuculidae

A small slender cuckoo, around the size of a bulbul. Female is brown with barred breast, wings and tail. Some females are of the rufous (or hepatic) morph, giving a reddish appearance.

location_on HK
Difficulty to photograph
When not singing, moves silently through the trees.

Sounds

Gives around 4 long notes followed by around 4 short notes, something like pi- pi- pi- pi- pipipipu. As such, it is also called the eight-note cuckoo (八聲杜鵑) in HK.
