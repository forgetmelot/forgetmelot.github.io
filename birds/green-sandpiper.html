Green Sandpiper

Tringa ochropus

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Scolopacidae

A dark brown sandpiper with slight mottling on the upperparts. Rump is white. Why "green", you ask? I have no idea. Greenish legs? The species name ochropus means "pale yellow feet". Greenish back, maybe...?
