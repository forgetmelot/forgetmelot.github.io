Black-winged Stilt

Himantopus himantopus

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Recurvirostridae

A tall, loud, white wader with black wings. While somewhat elegant, I find them slightly annoying because they call constantly about everything and scare all the other birds away.

Immature individuals are browner on the back. Some also have variable amounts of brown-black on the head and neck.

Its ridiculously long legs trail far behind in flight. In this flock, one of the birds seems to have a leg issue.
