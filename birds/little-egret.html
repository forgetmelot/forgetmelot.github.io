Little Egret

Egretta garzetta

Order: Pelecaniformes

Family: Ardeidae

An elegant white egret with a black bill, black legs and yellow toes. Did you know? A bird's "knees" are actually its ankles, its "feet" are its toes. That's why their "knees" bend the other way compared to our own.
