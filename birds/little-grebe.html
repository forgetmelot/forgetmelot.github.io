Little Grebe

Tachybaptus ruficollis

Order: Podicipediformes

Family: Podicipedidae

A tiny fluffy grebe. In breeding plumage, has a rufous neck and dark brown cap and body. Otherwise known as "dabchick". The genus name Tachybaptus describes its fast diving habits.

Some individuals have yellow, instead of dark, eyes. Dark eyes makes one look cute, yellow eyes... a bit stupid. A most respectable creature nonetheless.

Adults are small enough, but chicks are tinier! These bug-eyed critters will sit on their parents' backs for a ride.
