Streak-breasted Scimitar Babbler

Pomatorhinus ruficollis

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Timaliidae

A large brown babbler with a long decurved bill, white supercilium, and streaked breast. A ground hugger, occurs often below eye level.

location_on HK
Difficulty to photograph

Watching Tips

Occurs in mixed species feeding flocks, often with Rufous-capped Babbler and Huet's Fulvetta.
