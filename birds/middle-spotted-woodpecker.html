Middle Spotted Woodpecker

Dendrocoptes medius

Order: Piciformes

Family: Picidae

A medium-sized woodpecker with a smallish sharp bill, red crown and vent, back upperparts and white spots on the wings. Underparts are cream-coloured and streaked; Y-shaped black mark on the side of the neck.

location_on Germany
Difficulty to photograph
Less common than Great Spotted Woodpecker. Silent and rarely drums.
