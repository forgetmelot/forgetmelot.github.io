Northern Pintail

Anas acuta

Order: Anseriformes

Family: Anatidae

A long-necked dabbling duck that looks really smooth. Male has chocolate brown head and neck. A white patch creeps up the neck from the body. Body is vermiculated and grey. Long tail feathers gives the name "Pintail".

Female has the same overall shape, but is brown.
