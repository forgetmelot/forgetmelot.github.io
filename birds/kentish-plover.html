Kentish Plover

Anarhynchus alexandrinus

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Charadriidae

Tiny plover. White collar, black eye line and black (broken) breast band. Breeding male is rufous on the back of the head. Unfortunately it does not breed in the UK anymore despite its name, and is now a rare migrant. Moves impressively fast on sand. What are they running from...?
