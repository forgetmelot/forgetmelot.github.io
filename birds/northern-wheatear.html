Northern Wheatear

Oenanthe oenanthe

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Muscicapidae

A wheatear of open rocky terrain. Male has a grey back, black mask and orangey breast. The name "wheatear" is derived from Old English hwit (white) and ærs (arse/rump), referring to the white vent. Unfortunately this is the only image I have.
