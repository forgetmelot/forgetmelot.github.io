White-Throated Kingfisher

Halcyon smyrnensis

A reddish brown kingfisher with a white throat, red bill and bright blue wings and tail. It is a common misconception that kingfishers must "fish". Many species are actually forest dwellers that eat insects, frogs, lizards and small mammals even. The White-Throated does eat fish, alongside other things like the cockroach (?) we see in this image.
