Pallid Swift

Apus pallidus

A medium-sized swift with a pale throat. Rocks medium to pale brown plumage; I do not personally consider it particularly pallid. But it is nonetheless paler than Common Swift. Identified by its overall pale appearance, the most distinguishing feature being pale-edged "scales" along the main body, though this is subject to lighting conditions.

location_on Spain
