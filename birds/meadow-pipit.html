Meadow Pipit

Anthus pratensis

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Motacillidae

An LBJ of open country with a black-streaked breast. Gives a hint of a white eye ring, and a whitish submoustachial stripe flanked by black moustachial and malar stripes. Terrifyingly long hind claws for hanging out on the ground.
