Eastern Red-Rumped Swallow

Cecropis daurica

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Hirundinidae

A dark-backed swallow with pale streaked underparts and a black collar.

"Red" rump is an attractive golden flash that is absent in Barn Swallows.
