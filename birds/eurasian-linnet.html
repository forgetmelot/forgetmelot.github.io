Eurasian Linnet

Linaria cannabina

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Fringillidae

A brown, inconspicuous finch with white eye crescents. Found in open country and farmlands. Male has more red on the head and breast.

Female is overall brown.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
Male perches on conspicuous spots to sing. However, large feeding flocks can be found on the ground and obscured by grass.
