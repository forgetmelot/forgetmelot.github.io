Yellow-Crested Cockatoo

Cacatua sulphurea

Order: Psittaciformes

Family: Cacatuidae

A white cockatoo with a yellow crest. Yellow wash on cheeks and vent. Social, loud and conspicuous. HK has ~10% of the world's population of this IUCN Critically Endangered species.

location_on HK
Difficulty to photograph
Perched conspicuously in trees, though it's hard to get a good angle sometimes.
