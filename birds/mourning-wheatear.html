Mourning Wheatear

Oenanthe lugens

Other names: Maghreb Wheatear

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Muscicapidae

A wheatear found on rocky slopes. Male is whitish with a black face, wings and tail.

Juvenile is brown with black wings and tail.
