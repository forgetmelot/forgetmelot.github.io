Wood Lark

Lullula arborea

Other names: Woodlark

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Alaudidae

An LBJ of open habitats with trees. I don't have much to say about it, for I had only found it when I reviewed the photos. I have been looking for it for so long, but I thought it was a linnet or something! The supercilium extending to the nape, and short stumpy tail are good field marks.
