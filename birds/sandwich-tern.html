Sandwich Tern

Thalasseus sandvicensis

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Laridae

A medium-large tern with a black cap. Long black bill has a yellow tip. Not sure if it likes sandwiches; it is named after Sandwich in Kent, England, where the specimens for this species were first collected.
