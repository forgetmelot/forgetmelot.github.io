Yellow-breasted Bunting

Emberiza aureola

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Emberizidae

A critically endangered large bunting. Has brown upperparts and yellow underparts. In non-breeding plumage, male is overall very yellow and has brown markings on the face. Found in rice fields and open country. Used to be abundant, but was hunted to the brink to make a delicacy in southern China.
