Yellow-Legged Gull

Larus michahellis

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Laridae

A gull with grey wings, black wing-tips, yellow legs and a yellow bill with a red spot.

location_on Switzerland
Difficulty to photograph
Chills by the shore.

Identification tips

Look out for its leg and wing colour. It has yellow legs (pink in Herring Gull) and medium-grey wings (darker slate-grey in Lesser Black-Backed Gull).
