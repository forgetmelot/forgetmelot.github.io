Long-Tailed Shrike

Lanius schach

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Laniidae

A small, cute, masked bird with an orangey body and long tail. While adorable, this is the "butcherbird" that impales its prey on thorns. I would love to see it hunt one day.

A melanistic morph also exists.
