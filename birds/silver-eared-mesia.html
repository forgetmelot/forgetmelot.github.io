Silver-Eared Mesia

Leiothrix argentauris

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Leiothrichidae

A small, loud and colourful babbler. Has a yellow body, black cap, silvery cheek, red wingbars. Occurs in flocks in wooded habitats. HK birds seem to have descended from escaped pets.

location_on HK
Difficulty to photograph
Moves constantly overhead in the canopy, hard to get a clear shot or a good angle.
