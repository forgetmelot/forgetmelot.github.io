Silver-Eared Mesia

Leiothrix argentauris

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Leiothrichidae

A small colourful bird. Yellow body, black cap, silvery cheek, red wingbars. Occurs in flocks in wooded habitats.

Absurdly hard to photograph in the correct angle (so far I still have not succeeded).
