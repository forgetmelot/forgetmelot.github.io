Smew

Mergellus albellus

Order: Anseriformes

Family: Anatidae

Trust me, I have seen this bird!!! Male (first on the left) is white with dark lores and a black patch at the back of the crest. Female (first on the right) has a greyish body and rufous head with a white cheek. This group is just too far away to get a good photo even with a good iPhone.
