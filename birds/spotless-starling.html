Spotless Starling

Sturnus unicolor

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Sturnidae

Essentially European Starling, but well, spotless. Adults have dark iridescent plumage, without white tips on feathers (which gives the spotted appearance). Range restricted to the Iberian Peninsula and North Africa, where it is more abundant than European Starling.
