European Starling

Sturnus vulgaris

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Sturnidae

A small iridescent starling that shines with green and purple. Has white spots on the tips of feathers. Good at mimicking sounds.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
Occurs in loud flocks. Sometimes feeds on the grass in close proximity. Common to some gardens and park spaces.
