Sanderling

Calidris alba

Order: Charadriiformes

Family: Scolopacidae

Small, plump wader that sparks joy. In non-breeding plumage, upperparts are grey and underparts pristine white. Bill is straight and black. Legs are black and lacks a hind toe. Found foraging on the shore in groups, moving with sprightly steps.

In breeding plumage, upperparts become golden and black.
