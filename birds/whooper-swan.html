Whooper Swan

Cygnus cygnus

Order: Anseriformes

Family: Anatidae

A large white swan with a yellow and black bill. Unlike Mute Swan, neck looks very straight.

location_on Europe
Difficulty to photograph
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
It depends on where you are. In the UK, they are found in large flocks, roosting on water or feeding in fields, giving distant views, and are often afraid of humans. In Iceland, they behave like urban Mute Swans, eating food out of one's hand. Averages 2 stars.
