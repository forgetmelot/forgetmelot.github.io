Eurasian Sparrowhawk

Accipiter nisus

Order: Accipitriformes

Family: Accipitridae

A small raptor, around the size of a pigeon. Has barred underparts and wings; tail is square and wings rounded. Female is mostly brown while male has rusty cheeks and breast. Usually hunts through ambush. One's most likely encounter with a sparrowhawk is when it crashes into a bush while all the small birds scatter.

Difficulty to photograph
Hides in shrubbery while chilling. Hard to find after an ambush.
