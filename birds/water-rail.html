Water Rail

Rallus aquaticus

Order: Gruiformes

Family: Rallidae

An elusive rail with streaked brown upperparts, and slate grey face and breast. Black belly has broken white bars. Long red bill and long toes. Found in marshy areas, coming out for a few seconds then dipping back into the vegetation.
