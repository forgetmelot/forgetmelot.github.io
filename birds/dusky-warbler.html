Dusky Warbler

Phylloscopus fuscatus

Order: Passeriformes

Family: Phylloscopidae

A leaf warbler that is a mixture of brown (e.g. #996328), yellow (e.g. #E3BC59) and green (e.g. #95A15D). Occurs in open country and marshy areas. Reminds me somewhat of a Chiffchaff but maybe browner and with a more prominent eye stipe.
