Common Wood Pigeon

Columba palumbus

Order: Columbiformes

Family: Columbidae

A thick, grey pigeon with white neck and wing patches and a rosy wash on the breast. Has strange yellow eyes with pupils that are neither completely round nor centred properly.

Juvenile lacks the white neck patch.

Pigeons come close enough to showcase the nictitating membrane, i.e. "third eyelid", of birds. One might also see it on crows or raptors, where their eyes become white for a moment.
