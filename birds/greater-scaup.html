Greater Scaup

Aythya marila

Order: Anseriformes

Family: Anatidae

A diving duck. Male has a dark head which produces a green (but never purple) sheen in good lighting; has dark flanks and a vermiculated back that appears grey from a distance. Both sexes have staring yellow eyes, and dive often, only to emerge later near the original spot.

location_on UK
Difficulty to photograph
In itself not hard to photograph, but takes some effort to pick out from huge flocks of similar and more common species such as Tufted Duck.
